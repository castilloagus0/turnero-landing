# Turnate business context

Turnate is an appointment-management SaaS for businesses and professionals. Its capabilities include online reservations, scheduling and availability management, appointment administration, automatic WhatsApp and email reminders, fewer forgotten appointments and absences, less manual coordination, customer self-booking, and centralized reservation management.

Do not position Turnate as a barbershop-only product. It can serve barbershops, hair salons, beauty and aesthetics centers, consultories, independent professionals, health, spas, wellness, and any appointment-based business.

Write concise, commercial, human, and clear Spanish. Avoid excessive technical language, buzzwords, generic AI copy, and unnecessarily long paragraphs. Center messaging on less manual work, greater organization, fewer forgotten appointments, and a better customer experience.
